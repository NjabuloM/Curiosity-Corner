# Things I Used to Misunderstand

A record of where my mental model was wrong. These entries are some of the most useful in the repo, because each one shows a gap that reading alone didn't reveal.

Add an entry whenever a guess turns out wrong, a prediction fails, or something I "knew" turns out to be a simplification.

## Format

```markdown
### <short title>

- **What I thought:**
- **What's actually true:**
- **Why I believed it:** (an oversimplified explanation? a misleading analogy? never checked?)
- **How I found out:** (link to the question file or source)
```
